TC002C Duo discovery index
=========================
Updated 2026-10-06. Detailed evidence, command paths, caveats and experiment
restoration records live in docs/hardware-research.txt. This index preserves
side leads as well as the automatic-brightness/contrast investigation.
"Verified" applies to this device/firmware and the stated experiment scope.

Gamma / boost live follow-up (2026-10-06)
----------------------------------------
Dedicated FEf109 gamma25/75 acknowledged but showed no expected preview effect.
Writing its native curves to the main206110 composite LUT DOES change preview
midtones strongly; neutral rebuilt through the normal path.257 volatile commands
per update need rendering-safe scheduling and preservation of other curve stages
before product integration. Camera settings/stored ISP restored; no AGC freeze.
Boost route is class78/sub31 (correction to the earlier legacy label). Setter
emits success THEN fall-through error, and stages AFTER rebuilding. Consume both
responses, apply twice. Four-state and on/off trials completed with restoration,
but scene perturbations/drift prevent a reliable claimed visible boost effect.

Static repeat and Camera-menu integration (2026-10-06)
A later stationary-scene repeat152507 resolves the earlier confound: boost3 adds
about9 levels (5%) to preview p99-p1 span on both repeats; cleanup exact.
Camera gamma adjustment (50 neutral) and tone boost now have production controls.
Gamma targets the main206110 LUT, preserves preset/contrast/boost stages, and
uploads incrementally with progress/cancel. Boost is checkbox0/3 with double
apply and both replies consumed. Neither is an automatic contrast freeze.
Actual production API trial153423 changed settled preview meanY from63 to43
(gamma25) and126 (gamma75), with257 frames during each11s upload. All SDK blocks
and stored ISP verified after cleanup; saved selection reloads next startup.
